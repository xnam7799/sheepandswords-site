Sheep & Swords — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Sheep & Swords ("the game") is developed by Xuan Nam Pham.

Data we collect: none

The game does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings are saved only on your own device so you can continue playing. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the game.

Notifications

If you allow it, the game schedules local reminders on your device (for example, when your storage is full or a new day begins in your village). No notification data is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn this off at any time in iOS Settings.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
