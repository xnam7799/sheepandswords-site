FAQ

  • Where is my progress saved? On your device. Deleting the game deletes your progress.
  • How do I unlock Survival mode? Clear Chapter 1 of the Campaign.
  • The game asks for notifications — do I need them? No. They are optional reminders and can be turned off in iOS Settings.
